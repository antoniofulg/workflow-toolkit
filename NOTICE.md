# Workflow Toolkit notices

The Workflow Toolkit source pack is project-owned MIT-licensed source unless a skill's frontmatter
states otherwise. The distributed `wtk` skills are self-contained directories for use with a
project's skill installer; this repository does not install host-project files.

The integrated Lean skill is an adaptation of Tech Leads Club's `tlc-spec-lean`. Its reviewed
source URL, commit, content hash, and license are recorded in
[`.agents/skills/wtk-lean/.skill-meta.json`](.agents/skills/wtk-lean/.skill-meta.json); attribution
is retained in [`.agents/skills/wtk-lean/NOTICE.md`](.agents/skills/wtk-lean/NOTICE.md).

Project-owned skill adaptations retain their per-file `CC-BY-4.0` frontmatter.

Security lifecycle, Ponytail, adaptive-guidelines, and repository-intelligence tools are optional
companion projects. Their licenses and provenance are owned by their respective repositories and
are not bundled here.
